About LEAF bike

Power . Motion . Freedom

LEAF bike represents a greener way to move. “LEAF” symbolizes nature, freshness, and sustainability, while “BIKE” stands for mobility, freedom, and exploration. As an electric bike rental company, we aim to make every journey more eco-friendly, accessible, and enjoyable, encouraging a cleaner and greener future.
